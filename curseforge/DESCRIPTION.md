# Microphone! (Simple Voice Chat)

Let people at the back hear you. Craft a microphone, hold it in either hand, and right-click before you speak. It works with your usual Simple Voice Chat controls, whether you're calling a race or showing a group around a build.

**Sneak + right-click** to cycle through **64, 128, 192 and 256 blocks**. Listeners hear your voice from your position as it fades with distance. The mic remembers its range when you put it away.

## Crafting

At a crafting table, put a copper ingot in the top middle, iron–redstone dust–iron across the middle, and an iron ingot in the bottom middle. Each craft makes one microphone. Survival players can use it without an operator granting access.

## Server and event controls

- Adjust all four ranges in `config/microphone.json`. Crafting is on by default. Turn off crafting alone, or disable handheld amplification and run staff announcements only.
- Run an announcement with `/eventvoice`. Staff can choose a local range or explicitly go global across dimensions.
- Mute the audience, open the floor to one guest, or deafen listeners during a break. Voice groups, whispers and individual volume controls keep their usual roles.

## Installation

Install Microphone!, Fabric API **0.165.2+26.3**, and Simple Voice Chat **2.6.27+26.3** on the server **and every client**. Use Minecraft Java **26.3**, Java **25**, and Fabric Loader **0.20.1**.
